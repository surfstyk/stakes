How it works

Pick a promise

One thing you keep meaning to do. Move every day, phone down, lights out, whatever it is. Day one costs nothing.

Keep the day

Check in once, any time before midnight. No camera, no step counter, nobody grading you.

Keep the money

Make it official with a little NIM. Finish the run and it comes back with a bonus. Miss a day and that day's slice is gone.

Don't take our word for it.

Every check-in is written to the Nimiq blockchain from your own wallet. Not to a database we control. The whole record is public, and you can read it without asking us for anything.

Nobody is watching you.

There is no camera, no tracker, and no model deciding whether you really did it. That is deliberate. The moment something becomes the referee, keeping your word stops being trust and starts being compliance. So the money is handled by systems, and the truth is handled by you.
