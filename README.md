# Stakes

> **Put your money where your mouth is — with your crew.**

**Stakes** is a **social commitment game** built as a [Nimiq Pay](https://www.nimiq.com/nimiq-pay/) Mini App. You stake a small amount on a personal goal for a week, do it daily — kept honest by your friends and social proof, *not* surveillance — and reclaim your stake plus a share of what the quitters forfeit (plus a little NIM) if you follow through. Miss a day, forfeit that day's slice.

Built for the **[Nimiq Mini Apps Competition](https://miniappscompetition.com/)**, Cycle I (build window **6 Jul – ~2 Aug 2026**).

**Status:** Concept locked. No application code yet — this repo currently baselines the research and planning from the kickoff session (Jun 2026).

## Documentation
Detailed design, strategy, and planning documents are maintained privately by the team and are not part of this public repository.

## Two ideas at the core
- *The money's job is to be **at risk**, not to be **won**.* The stake is working capital for your willpower.
- *A commitment game built on **trust, not surveillance**.* Money is handled trustlessly by a smart contract; honesty is handled by your friends.
